# @weight 2

nc as busybox's netcat 1.10.  Each case runs nc in a forked child, its
standard input a file holding the case's input, its standard output and
error captured; the peer is a second child.  A client case's peer
listens on the case's port, P in its arguments, sends its reply a fifth
of a second after the connection (as late as the oracle's peer, so nc has
sent its input first), shuts
its write side and records what arrives; a listen case's peer connects
to that port, sends "hi\n", shuts its write side and records the
answer.  -o's dump follows stdout, each line after "hex " -- the runner
reads a line opening "> " as a prompt.  Every expectation is busybox's own
output for the same peer and
arguments; the report gives stdout, stderr, the status and what the
peer received, the port spelled PORT.
